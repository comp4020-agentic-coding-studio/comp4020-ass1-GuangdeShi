八字 · Bāzì · Four Pillars of Destiny

A moment in time becomes
four layers,
and eight characters.

Chinese calendrical tradition reads a single instant on four scales at once — year, month, day, hour. Each becomes a column of two characters. The four scales do not turn over together, and that is the interesting part: change the moment and watch which columns move.

生時 The birth moment

Read as China Standard Time (UTC+8), the meridian Chinese almanacs are calculated for.

An explainer of a calendrical system, not a fortune-telling service. Nothing here predicts anything.

Solar terms are computed from the Sun's apparent longitude to about ±15 minutes. Where schools of Bazi genuinely disagree — timezone, the day boundary, branch polarity — the choice made is named in the source at src/bazi/calculate.ts rather than hidden.
